1  Introduction

1.1 Purpose

SiamMart today runs returns and refunds on a fragile manual chain: paper/Excel at the branch, a LINE group to HQ, and manual refunds in SAP every Friday. Refund lead time averages 14 days (peer benchmark 3 days) and slow refunds are the company's #1 social-media complaint. This document specifies a single, auditable returns system for Phase 1 that consolidates every return request, applies policy and approval rules, drives refunds into SAP and tracks returned goods — built as production-ready requirements that developers, testers and SiamMart can sign off on.

1.2 Audience & use

  • Developer / tester team (TK) — build and test directly from TR / NFR / INT / FS / US and their acceptance criteria.
  • Solution architect (TK) — reuses the TR-/NFR-/INT- IDs verbatim in the architecture pack.
  • SiamMart approvers (K.Somchai, K.Pim, K.Arthit) — review, resolve OI- items and sign off.

1.3 Scope of this document

This is the requirements package (Output 2). The matching solution architecture & workflow pack is TK-SA-SMR-ARC-01. Everything here traces to the kickoff notes (input.md); nothing has been invented. Where the notes are silent or contradictory, a requirement is written to the safest interpretation and the gap is logged as an AS- or OI-.

1.4 Document status

Status: Draft v1.0 — issued for senior-BA verification and customer review. Open issues (OI-01…OI-08) must be resolved before functional sign-off. Assumptions (AS-01…AS-11) must be confirmed before build freeze. No requirement marked Must is considered final until its blocking OI- is closed.

2  Business Context & Objectives

2.1 As-Is process (today)

SiamMart is a retail chain of ~120 สาขา plus an online shop (own website + Shopee / Lazada). Returns arrive through three disconnected paths, all of which converge on manual, person-dependent handling:

ChannelToday (as observed in kickoff)Pain
In-store returnCustomer returns at the counter → staff writes it on paper (some branches use Excel) → photo to the LINE group “Return-HQ” → HQ CS reviews and approves inside LINE.No system of record; approvals lost in chat; re-keying errors.
Online returnCustomer emails returns@siammart.co.th or opens chat → CS copies details into the shared-drive spreadsheet “RETURN_MASTER_v7_final_FINAL.xlsx”.One fragile spreadsheet; version chaos; no audit trail.
Refund (finance)HQ-approved returns are refunded manually in SAP by Finance, batched every Friday.Backlog; refunds take 14 days on average (10–21 day range), peers do 3 days.
Returned goodsReturned stock piles behind the store; no rule on restock vs. return-to-DC/supplier vs. write-off.Inventory loss, stock shrinkage, unknown value.

Consequence: slow refunds are the #1 complaint on social media last quarter (~2,300 complaints — to be confirmed, AS-04). There is also no visibility of fraud (repeat returners) and no management reporting on return volume, reason or branch.

The real business problem (why)

The customer's literal ask is “one system where everything is in one place.” Drilled down, the measurable outcome is: cut refund lead time from ~14 days to ≤3 days, stop the manual spreadsheets/LINE approvals, and give Finance and executives an auditable, reconciled view — without increasing fraud or losing returned stock.

  • Customer retention: moving from 14→3 days removes the #1 complaint driver and protects repeat revenue.

  • Fraud: repeat returners become visible, protecting margin.

5  Business Rules (cont.)

5.2 Rule-governance notes

BRL-001 vs BRL-002 are mutually exclusive and unresolved. They encode the K.Somchai / K.Pim conflict. The system must support both as configuration so that whichever way OI-01 is decided, it is a configuration change, not a re-build. Phase-1 default until decided: BRL-002 (all refunds to Finance) — the more conservative, audit-safe option.

Why default to the conservative option

Choosing auto-approval before Finance agrees risks an audit finding and re-work; choosing Finance-approval only costs some speed but is reversible via configuration. OI-01 must still be formally closed with the customer before functional sign-off.
