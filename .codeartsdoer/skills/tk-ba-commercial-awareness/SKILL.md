# TK BA — Commercial Awareness

A TK BA protects both the customer relationship and TK's margin.

- **Size the value** – state each Must requirement's business value (time saved, complaints reduced, revenue protected). Quote customer numbers; mark unverified ones.
- **Budget fit** – compare scope against the stated budget. If Must items clearly exceed it, propose a phased plan rather than silently cutting.
- **Scope creep guard** – every "เพิ่มนิดเดียว" after sign-off goes through a **Change Request (CR)** with impact on cost, time and risk.
- **Hidden costs** – third-party licences, SMS cost per message, marketplace API fees, data migration, hypercare, MA/hosting (also a recurring-revenue opportunity for TK).
- **Risky commitments** – fixed go-live dates tied to events (e.g. 11.11) need buffer + contingency; record in risks.
- **Upsell, don't oversell** – "nice to have" ideas (e.g. AI image inspection) go in *Future Opportunities*, never committed scope.
- **Internal vs customer copy** – the *Commercial Notes* section is TK-internal. Remove it before the document goes to the customer.
